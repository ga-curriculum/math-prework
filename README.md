## About

Welcome to our Math Pre-Work! In just over three hours, this mini course will provide you with a solid foundation in math essentials for Data Science. You'll learn essential concepts such as random variables, basic stats, central limit theorem, vectors and matrix operations in python. Designed for beginners, this program is your first step towards learning the essential math skills and preparing for more advanced concepts later. Dive in and start your mathematical journey here!
